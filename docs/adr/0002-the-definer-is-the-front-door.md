# The Definer is the front door

2026-09-08

Henok brings everything to the Definer: ideas, defects, small asks, findings after a round closed, tickets other people opened that he wants worked, and the order of the next round. The Definer defines, publishes, labels `ready-for-agent` and writes the manifest with him, so there is one place where work is shaped and one person who chooses the order. The Orchestrator hears only "go", "close the round", "emergency" and answers to the lines it wrote in "for you"; anything else gets a one-line answer: take it to the Definer.

Considered options: the Orchestrator as the front door, rejected because it chooses again the moment Henok is vague.
