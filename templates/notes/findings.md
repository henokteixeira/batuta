# findings

One line each: what showed up and does not belong to the ticket in hand. Nothing here is dispatched or labelled in the round it appeared, however urgent. At round close the Orchestrator marks each line `⇒ candidate` or `⇒ recommend discard`; only the Definer, with Henok, turns a line into a ticket or discards it, and deletes the line then. A production emergency is not a finding: it is one line in "for you".
