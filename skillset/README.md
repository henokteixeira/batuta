# Batuta Nori skillset

Henok's skillset: Nori's execution core, Matt Pocock's definition skills, and the Maestri skills, with the global `AGENTS.md` that governs all of them. It is linked into Nori as `personal/batuta` and switched on with `nori-skillsets switch personal/batuta`.

The skills say how an agent works a slice; they do not say what runs. That is the round: a manifest of at most three tickets the Definer closed with Henok, dispatched by the Orchestrator, implemented by executors in parallel worktrees, merged by Henok himself.

Read `../README.md` for the repository, and `../docs/workflow.md` for how a round runs.
